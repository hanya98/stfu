# SStack brand direction

## Provisional recommendation

Use **SStack** as the working name. This brief’s second direction was described with the “Stack the Front Up” line; this document treats its name as SStack, as signalled by the brief title. The tagline is refined to **“Stack your prep. Ship your progress.”** “Front Up” can sound frontend-specific, while the product is for broader DSA and interview preparation.

| Criterion | STFU — “Stop Thinking, Finish Up” | SStack — “Stack the Front Up” |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memorability | Very high; immediately noticeable | High; a distinctive developer-style construction |
| Portfolio credibility | Risky in a résumé, interview, or professional conversation | More professional and easier to present |
| DSA/interview fit | The execution idea fits, but “stop thinking” can imply rushing or shallow learning | Stacking practice and skills fits sustained preparation |
| Broader product fit | Tied to a provocative productivity slogan | More extensible across developer learning and workflow tools |
| Pronunciation/discovery | Spoken as letters, but the common profanity meaning dominates searches | Usually said “S-stack”; spelling needs a capital S to be clear |
| Confusion/reputation | Strong reputational risk because “STFU” has an established hostile meaning | “Stack” is common developer vocabulary; “Front Up” risks suggesting frontend-only work |
| Visual identity | Bold initials, but difficult to use without the profanity reading | Layered stack mark, monogram, and structured-progress motif work at small sizes |

**Decision:** SStack is the safer provisional direction. Use a stacked-layer symbol and violet accent. The “Stop Thinking, Finish Up” phrase is memorable but its profanity association and negative learning implication outweigh that benefit for a student-facing preparation product. The suggested “Stack the Front Up” tagline is not retained verbatim because of the frontend-only reading; the copy is centralized in `src/lib/brand.ts` so it can be revised consistently.

This is a product and communication assessment only. **No trademark, domain, or social-handle availability has been checked or implied.** Verify those before committing to a public launch or investing in final brand assets.
